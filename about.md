I'm **Ian Guidry**, a cybersecurity analyst. I got into security after someone knocked my Xbox offline with a DDoS attack and I needed to know how. That curiosity turned into a career.

## What I do

- **SIEM and log analysis:** turning raw logs into detections that mean something
- **Investigations:** following suspicious activity from the first alert to the root cause
- **Vulnerability assessments:** finding weaknesses before someone else does
- **Detection engineering:** building, testing and tuning the alerts that catch attacks

## What I write about

Hands-on labs and projects: what I built, what broke, and what I learned. Expect posts on cloud security, DevSecOps pipelines, detection and response, and the occasional help desk tool.
