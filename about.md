## What I work on

My projects focus on the day-to-day work of a security analyst:

- **SIEM and log analysis:** turning raw logs into detections that mean something
- **Investigations:** following suspicious activity from the first alert to the root cause
- **Detection engineering:** building, testing and tuning alerts, then attacking my own labs to prove they fire
- **Cloud and pipeline security:** scanning, signing and policy-checking releases before they run
